title: Checkpoint Catalog
---

Checkpoints are structured deliverables where Tech Leads engage with a specific dimension of open source product development. You will complete four **Artifact Choice** checkpoints during the semester. Browse this catalog to understand each option before selecting.

| Checkpoint | Recommended | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Community Building](/courses/89519/pages/community-building) | Sprint 3 | Execute a community-building initiative and reflect on what you learned about your project's community needs and strategy. |
| [User-Focused Checkpoint](/courses/89519/pages/user-focused-checkpoint) | Sprint 4 | Conduct user research—interviews, usability testing, or prototype testing—to validate whether your work actually helps real users accomplish their goals. |
| [System Designs](/courses/89519/pages/system-designs) | Any | Create visual design artifacts (architecture diagrams, wireframes, data models, user flows) that communicate your system's structure or behavior. |
| [Strategy Draft](/courses/89519/pages/strategy-draft) | Sprints 5–6 | Draft your Product Strategy and Community Strategy documents, then receive feedback before producing final polished versions. |
| [Threat Model](/courses/89519/pages/threat-model) | Any | Systematically identify security risks for your product, assess their impact and likelihood, and propose appropriate mitigations. |
| [Backlog Audit](/courses/89519/pages/backlog-audit) | Sprints 6–7 | Audit and clean up your project backlog to prepare it for future teams and external contributors. |

*Detailed instructions for each checkpoint are linked above and in the Checkpoints module.*
