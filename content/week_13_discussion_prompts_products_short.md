# Week 13 Discussion Prompts: Products

1. Where does your product end, and what crept in this semester that probably shouldn't have?
2. What language did you inherit with your codebase, and has it cost you anything when your team or client meant different things by the same word?
3. When did strong conviction serve you well this semester, when did it lead you astray, and did you have a reliable way to tell the difference in the moment?
4. What was the most effective thing you did to make your product vision concrete for your team or your client, and what stayed too abstract for too long?
5. Your product sits at the intersection of your team, your client, and your users, each with a different model of what the product is. Where has something gotten lost in translation?
6. Be honest: did you sustain any discovery practices this semester, or did delivery pressure collapse everything into "build what's on the board"?
7. Who or what taught you the most about product thinking this semester? Name it specifically.
8. What do you understand about building products now that you didn't understand in January?
