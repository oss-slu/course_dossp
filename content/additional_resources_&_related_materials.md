Resources & Related Material

## Resources

This material is optional, but can be highly valuable in some contexts. Let me know if you have any questions about any of these.

## Team Dynamics: 

- *Agile Coaching *by Rachel Davies and Liz Sedley
- *Team Geek* by Brian W. Fitzpatrick, Ben Collins-Sussman
- *Debugging Teams*, Ben Collins-Sussman, Brian Fitzpatrick. O'Reilly Media, Inc. 2015. https://www.debuggingteams.com/
- *Leaders Eat Last: Why Some Teams Pull Together and Others Don't *by Simon Sinek (2016 talk at Microsoft Research)

## Community Management: 

- *DIY Citizenship: Critical Making and Social Media. *Edited by Matt Ratto, Megan Boler.
- *The Open Source Way 2.0. *Karsten Wade, et. al. https://www.theopensourceway.org/
- *The Art of Community, Second Edition*. Jono Bacon. O'Reilly Media, Inc. 20012. https://www.jonobacon.com/books/artofcommunity/

## Software Engineering: 

- *97 Things Every Programmer Should Know *by Kevlin Henney.
- *The Art of Doing Science and Engineering*. Richard W. Hamming. Stripe Press, 2020. https://press.stripe.com/the-art-of-doing-science-and-engineering
- *Continuous Delivery: Reliable Software Releases Through Build, Test, and Deployment Automation* by Dave Farley, Jez Humble
- *Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code *by Martin Fowler, Kent Beck

## Product Management:

- *Working Backwards: Insights, Stories, and Secrets from Inside Amazon *by Colin Bryar, Bill Carr
- *The Product Manager's Handbook* by Linda Gorchels
- *Continuous Discovery Habits: Discover Products That Create Customer Value and Business Value. Teresa Torres. Product Talk, LLC, 2021. https://www.producttalk.org/2021/05/continuous-discovery-habits/*
