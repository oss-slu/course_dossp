## Overview

This is the final performance evaluation for the semester. It is intentionally scheduled at the end of finals week to allow you to put final touches on your projects before your work is assessed.

This evaluation follows the same professional-style approach as previous evaluations, with one key difference: **this is the only evaluation where your Demonstrate Professional Leadership assignment directly factors into your grade**.

## Weight Distribution (100 points total)

- 35% (35 points) - Managing Team Dynamics
- 35% (35 points) - Software Engineering Quality
- 15% (15 points) - Product and Project Management
- 15% (15 points) - Implementing Open Source Practices
- 10% (10 points) - Demonstrate Professional Leadership

## Matrix Evaluation

Performance is evaluated using a matrix that crosses **holistic performance factors** with **evidence-based factors**.

### Holistic Performance Factors (Rows)

| Factor | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Managing Team Dynamics | 35% | Communication, collaboration, client relationships, retrospectives, inclusive culture, conflict resolution |
| Software Engineering Quality | 35% | Architecture, maintainability, testing, code review culture, CI/CD, technical documentation |
| Product and Project Management | 15% | Vision articulation, discovery, project management tools, checkpoint documentation, sprint execution |
| Implementing Open Source Practices | 15% | License compliance, documentation, community governance, maintainer responsiveness |
| Demonstrate Professional Leadership | 10% | Leadership capacity building activities as documented in the Demonstrate Professional Leadership assignment |

### Evidence-Based Factors (Columns)

| Evidence Source | Description |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Sprint Feedback & Merges | Code review activity, PR merges, team velocity, sprint close reports |
| Checkpoint Artifacts | Team working agreements, artifact choices, milestone documentation |
| Good Practices | OKRs, milestone definitions, retrospective facilitation, metrics reports |
| Class Discussion Participation | Engagement in class discussions, knowledge sharing, peer support |
| Final Deliverables | Team presentation, working software, product strategy, community strategy |

Each cell in the matrix represents how evidence from that source demonstrates performance in that factor.

## Grade Expectations

### B-Level Performance

To earn a B, you must demonstrate one of the following:

- **Reaching your target Milestones** for each iteration, showing consistent progress toward team goals
- **Documenting adversity clearly** when factors outside your control prevented reaching milestones, including what you learned from the experience and how you adapted

Good-faith effort in the face of adversity is expected. If challenges arise, your response to those challenges matters as much as the outcome.

### A-Level Performance

A-level performance requires completing the Demonstrate Professional Leadership assignment, as it accounts for 10% of the overall course grade. Strong performance across all other factors combined with completed leadership activities demonstrates the comprehensive engagement expected for top marks.

## Assessment Philosophy

Evaluation incorporates instructor observation of all communications and codebase, peer feedback, client input, review of project management artifacts and checkpoint documents, and evidence of community engagement. Focus is on growth trajectory, learning from mistakes, and cultivating both technical excellence and human-centered leadership.

## Legacy Codebase Context

Tech Leads are often building on legacy codebases and inheriting existing product documentation and open source practices. Performance will be evaluated not just on the current state of artifacts, but on what you are actively doing to shape things toward a better state. The emphasis is on demonstrating improvement, strategic refactoring, and progressive enhancement of inherited systems.
