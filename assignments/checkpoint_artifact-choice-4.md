**Points Possible:** 1.0
**Target Sprint:** 6
**Focus:** Checkpoint Artifact Selection

## Overview

This assignment is for the submission of one of the four chosen **Artifact Checkpoints** selected from the Catalog. Tech Leads should view these checkpoints not as random tasks, but as concrete steps that directly inform and build the final product and community strategy. Successfully completing these tasks should make the overall final deliverable easier to achieve.

## Requirements

* **Catalog Artifact Submission:** Submit the primary artifact for the chosen Checkpoint Catalog item. The submission must be a publishable, fixed-format file (e.g., PDF, MP4, image, Markdown document). Multiple files may be uploaded if necessary to capture both documentation and multimedia evidence.
* **Artifact Identification and Rationale:** The submitted file(s) must be clearly labeled to identify which Checkpoint Catalog item it fulfills. Include a brief rationale (1-2 paragraphs) in the submission file or an accompanying document explaining why this specific checkpoint was selected as the most relevant and meaningful task for your team at this time, and how it contributes to the final product and community strategy.

## Deliverable

* **Artifact File(s)** - The evidence demonstrating completion of the selected checkpoint task.

## Evaluation

**Evaluation:** Substantive engagement—evidence that a relevant checkpoint task was meaningfully executed and documented, demonstrating appropriate effort for the chosen task. Relevance and meaningful effort are the standard of evaluation, as checkpoint tasks vary widely between projects.

## About the Deadline

**This is a target deadline.** It is set to evenly space out the workload throughout the semester. Tech Leads are professional members of the team and are free to submit this work as early or as late within the semester as is most appropriate for their team's workflow and needs.
