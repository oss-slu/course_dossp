## Overview

Establish Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) for your team.

## Purpose

OKRs provide a framework for setting and tracking team goals. They help align team efforts, create accountability, and make progress visible.

## What Are OKRs?

**Objectives** are qualitative goals that describe what you want to achieve. They should be meaningful, ambitious, and inspiring.

**Key Results** are quantitative metrics that measure progress toward objectives. They should be specific, measurable, and time-bound.

For a comprehensive introduction to OKRs, see John Doerr's *Measure What Matters* and the official OKR resources at [whatmatters.com](https://www.whatmatters.com/).

## Deliverable

Submit your team's OKRs, including:

- At least one Objective for the semester
- Two or more Key Results for each Objective
- A brief description of how you plan to track progress

## Flexibility

You decide what objectives matter to your team and what key results you want to track. The specific content of your OKRs is up to you - what matters is that you have a system for setting goals and measuring progress.

Examples of things teams might track:
- Code quality metrics
- Feature delivery velocity
- Documentation coverage
- Test coverage improvements
- Response time to issues/PRs
- Team satisfaction or health metrics
- Client feedback scores

## Evaluation

This is a completion-based good practice. Full credit is awarded for establishing meaningful OKRs and demonstrating a plan to track them. You are not evaluated on *what* you choose to track, but on *whether* you are tracking something meaningful to your team.
