## Overview

Present a comprehensive demonstration of your product updates to your client. This demo showcases the work completed during the first iteration and demonstrates the value delivered.

## Purpose

Client demos are a core practice in professional software development. They provide:

- Visibility into progress for stakeholders
- Opportunity for client feedback and course correction
- Practice communicating technical work to different audiences
- Evidence of value delivered

## Requirements

### Demo Content

Create a comprehensive review of product updates suitable for a client demo:

- **Duration**: 10-30 minutes
- **Scope**: Work completed since the last demo or project start
- **Focus**: Demonstrate the value of the work at a level of detail appropriate for your client
  - **Technical clients**: Include technical details, code architecture, implementation decisions
  - **Non-technical clients**: Focus on outcomes, user interfaces, business value

**Important**: This must be original demo content. You cannot resubmit developer sprint demos that were already submitted elsewhere.

### Demo Format

Demos can be delivered as:

- **Live online meeting** with client (preferred when client is available)
- **Pre-recorded demo** if client availability is limited

Pre-recorded demos are acceptable and should be prepared as if presenting to the client directly.

### Client Feedback

The instructor will solicit feedback from clients directly. Your responsibility is to deliver a quality demo experience.

## Deliverable

Submit:

1. **Demo recording**: Video of the live client meeting or pre-recorded demo
2. **Brief reflection**: A sentence or short paragraph reflecting on the demo experience - what went well, what you learned, how the client responded (if live)

## Evaluation

This is a completion-based good practice. Full credit is awarded for delivering a substantive client demo that appropriately communicates the value of work completed.
