## Overview

Define the product milestone your team is working towards for Iteration 2.

## Purpose

Milestones provide a clear target for iteration work. They help the team focus on delivering meaningful value and give stakeholders visibility into what to expect by the end of the iteration.

## Deliverable

Submit your Milestone 2 definition, including:

- **Milestone Name**: A concise title that captures the iteration goal
- **Description**: What the team aims to deliver by the end of Iteration 2
- **Success Criteria**: How you will know the milestone has been achieved
- **Key Deliverables**: The specific features, fixes, or artifacts that comprise this milestone

Your milestone should build on Iteration 1 progress and be ambitious but achievable given your team's capacity and the iteration timeline.

## Evaluation

This is a completion-based good practice. Full credit is awarded for defining a clear, meaningful milestone that provides direction for your team's iteration work.

Milestone progress will be evaluated as part of your performance evaluations. Reaching your target milestones (or documenting factors outside your control that prevented reaching them and what you learned) is required for B-level performance.
