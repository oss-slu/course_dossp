**Due:** End of Iteration (Sprint 7/Launch Sprint)
**Focus:** Client-Ready & Handoff-Ready Strategic Documentation

## Overview

Your Product Strategy is one of your most important deliverables. It synthesizes your semester's worth of continuous discovery, technical decision-making, and stakeholder collaboration into a document that serves multiple purposes:
- **Guides current decisions:** Provides a north star for prioritization and technical choices
- **Communicates vision:** Enables clients and stakeholders to understand your product direction
- **Enables handoff:** Equips future Tech Leads to continue building meaningfully
- **Demonstrates leadership:** Shows your ability to synthesize diverse inputs into coherent strategy
This document should be polished, professional, and ready to share with clients, future maintainers, and anyone evaluating your product's direction.

## Requirements

Create a comprehensive Product Strategy document using the template in Appendix C. Your strategy should:

### Synthesize Your Product Management Work

Include evidence of:
- Continuous discovery findings (user interviews, client conversations, usage analysis)
- Opportunity Solution Trees and how solutions address validated user opportunities
- Experimental learnings (what you tested, what worked, what didn't, and why)
- Technical insights and how architecture decisions serve user needs
- Stakeholder alignment across client needs, community input, program requirements, and technical vision

### Include All Required Sections

See Appendix C for the complete template. Key sections include:
- Strategic Foundation (executive summary, vision, problem statement)
- User Understanding (target users, needs, personas, research methods)
- Market Context (existing solutions, market opportunity, value proposition)
- Product Direction (roadmap, key features, what's not on roadmap)
- Technical Foundation (architecture, constraints, risks)
- Implementation Reality (go-to-market, success metrics, risk mitigation)

### Demonstrate Strategic Thinking

Strong Product Strategies:
- Lead with "why" - explain reasoning behind decisions
- Show trade-offs and acknowledge downsides
- Connect decisions to evidence from research and feedback
- Provide decision frameworks for future teams
- Document lessons learned
- Enable future maintainers to evolve the product coherently

### Meet Professional Standards

Your document should be well-organized, clearly written for multiple audiences, appropriately detailed, properly formatted, and error-free.

## Deliverables

## Evaluation Criteria

## Strategic Synthesis

- Evidence of continuous discovery informing strategy
- Clear connection between user needs and product decisions
- Thoughtful integration of client input, community feedback, and technical constraints

## User Understanding

- Validated user needs, not assumptions
- Clear personas and use cases grounded in research
- Compelling value proposition that differentiates from alternatives

## Technical-Product Integration

- Technical decisions explained in terms of user value
- Architecture that balances current needs with future flexibility
- Honest assessment of constraints and risks with realistic mitigation

## Handoff Quality

- Future teams can understand product direction and make consistent decisions
- Lessons learned documented
- Clear guidance for making future product choices

## Professional Quality

- Client-ready polish and presentation
- Clear, accessible writing
- Proper structure and formatting
