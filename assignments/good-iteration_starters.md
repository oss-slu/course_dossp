## Overview

This good practice recognizes and makes visible the planning and onboarding work Tech Leads do at the start of the semester.

## Purpose

Tech Leads are already onboarded to their projects, but the work of planning for the iteration and onboarding your team is substantial and often invisible. This assignment acknowledges that effort.

## What This Covers

### Iteration Planning

- Reviewing project state and priorities
- Setting up or refining the project backlog
- Defining initial sprint goals
- Preparing for client meetings and expectations

### Team Onboarding

- Welcoming and orienting new developers
- Ensuring development environment setup documentation is current
- Facilitating initial team discussions and introductions
- Establishing communication channels and norms
- Preparing developers to contribute in Sprint 1

## Deliverable

Submit a brief summary of your iteration start activities:

- What planning work you completed to prepare for the semester
- How you onboarded your team members
- Any challenges encountered and how you addressed them
- What's ready for Sprint 1

## Evaluation

This is a completion-based good practice. Full credit is awarded for demonstrating meaningful engagement with iteration planning and team onboarding responsibilities.
