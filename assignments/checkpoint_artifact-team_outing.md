**Points Possible:** 1.0

**Target Sprint:** 2

**Focus:** Team Dynamics

## Overview

Team cohesion is a critical component of successful software development. This checkpoint encourages Tech Leads and their teams to engage in a non-work-related activity to strengthen relationships, build trust, and improve team dynamics outside of the structured sprint environment. This is considered a required checkpoint, typically completed early in the semester (Sprint 2).

## Requirements

* **Substantive Engagement:** The activity must involve a significant portion of the team and require focused time and energy.
* **Documentation Format:** The documentation must be written in the style of a **LinkedIn status update or professional blog post** (1-2 paragraphs), highlighting what was done, when it occurred, and who participated.
* **Photo Consent:** Any submitted photos must be accompanied by explicit notes regarding consent:
  * **Option A:** Consent is granted for **assignment submission only.**
  * **Option B:** Consent is granted for **assignment submission AND public promotional use** (e.g., program blog, official LinkedIn posts).

## Recommendations

* **Activity Type:** Choose an activity that is accessible and enjoyable for all team members.
* **Inclusivity:** Ensure the activity is voluntary and respectful of all team members' schedules and personal preferences.

## Deliverables

* **Professional Status Update Documentation:** The text (written like a LinkedIn status update) detailing the activity, participant list, and date.
* **Evidence:** Photos or other suitable evidence demonstrating that the team activity took place, clearly labeled with the participant consent option (A or B).

## Evaluation

**Evaluation:** Substantive engagement—evidence that the team activity occurred and was documented. The evaluation focuses on the quality of documentation and evidence of participation.

## About the Deadline

*This is a target deadline.* Missing this deadline due to legitimate challenges or circumstances outside your control can demonstrate professionalism through how you communicate and adapt. Tech Leads should communicate any necessary adjustments to the instructor.

## Submission

Submit the professional status update documentation and supporting evidence, ensuring photo consent is clearly indicated.
