## Description

As an Instructor, I need to collect final feedback from clients and stakeholders to assess overall satisfaction, capture lessons learned, and inform future semesters.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Final feedback requests sent to all stakeholders
- [ ] Responses collected and documented
- [ ] Overall satisfaction assessed
- [ ] Lessons learned captured
- [ ] Feedback incorporated into course improvement

## Notes

Due: May 4, 2026 (wrap-up week)

**Purpose:** Capture comprehensive end-of-semester feedback for team recognition, course improvement, and stakeholder relationship continuity.

**Questions to ask stakeholders:**
- Overall, how satisfied are you with this semester's work? (Scale + open response)
- Did the team meet your expectations?
- How would you rate the overall communication and professionalism?
- What worked well that future teams should continue?
- What could be improved for future semesters?
- Would you recommend working with this program again?
- Would you be willing to continue with a new team next semester?

**Uses for this feedback:**
- Team recognition and final evaluations
- Course improvement for future semesters
- Stakeholder relationship management
- Case studies and testimonials (with permission)
- Informing next semester's team assignments

**Follow-up:**
- Thank stakeholders for their partnership
- Discuss continuation plans if applicable
- Request permission to use positive feedback publicly
