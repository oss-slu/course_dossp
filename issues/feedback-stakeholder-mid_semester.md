## Description

As an Instructor, I need to check in with clients and stakeholders mid-semester to assess communication regularity and overall satisfaction between demos.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Check-in requests sent to all stakeholders
- [ ] Responses collected and documented
- [ ] Communication patterns assessed
- [ ] Relationship health evaluated
- [ ] Issues escalated or addressed as needed

## Notes

Due: March 16, 2026 (between Demo 1 and Demo 2)

**Purpose:** Ensure regular stakeholder contact is happening and relationships remain healthy outside of formal demo checkpoints.

**Questions to ask stakeholders:**
- How frequently has your team been in contact since Demo 1?
- Are you satisfied with the communication cadence?
- Have your questions and requests been addressed promptly?
- How confident are you in the team's progress toward your goals?
- Any concerns we should address before Demo 2?

**Communication health indicators:**
- Regular updates (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Responsive to stakeholder questions
- Proactive about blockers or changes
- Clear about what's being worked on

**Red flags:**
- No contact since Demo 1
- Unanswered stakeholder requests
- Stakeholder confusion about current work
- Declining confidence in team
