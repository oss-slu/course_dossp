## Description

**Tech Leads** receive targeted coaching based on accumulated observations so that they enter Retrospective 1 with actionable development areas.

**Instructor** synthesizes Round 1 1:1s, sprint checks, and engagement data so that coaching interventions are specific and timely.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Engagement patterns reviewed across all 27 Tech Leads
- [ ] Tech Leads needing additional support identified
- [ ] Targeted coaching messages sent (DM or in-person)
- [ ] Positive progress acknowledged

## Stretch AC (Coaching Impact)

- [ ] All struggling Tech Leads receive direct outreach
- [ ] Coaching conversations result in concrete action items
- [ ] Tech Leads reference coaching in Retro 1 discussions
- [ ] Observable behavior change in subsequent weeks

## Notes

**Timing**: End of Round 1, before Retrospective 1 and Demo 1

**Data Sources**:
- Round 1 1:1 meeting notes
- Sprint check observations (weeks 1-5)
- GitHub activity patterns
- Slack engagement levels
- Reading discussion participation

**Coaching Focus Areas**:
- Team communication patterns
- Sprint close consistency
- Delegation and accountability
- Technical leadership visibility

This is deeper engagement than weekly sprint checks—synthesizing patterns and providing mentorship rather than surface-level observation.
